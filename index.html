Background

A developer who builds
with purpose, not just code.

Rudradutt Padhya — known as Rudra — is a self-taught web developer and entrepreneur from Siddhpur, Gujarat. His journey started with home tuitions after 12th Science, teaching students out of sheer necessity. That same grit later led him to build real digital products for real businesses.

Today, as the Founder of TurnAround Experts (TAE), Rudradutt helps startups and loss-making businesses rebuild — providing web development, strategy, and hands-on execution. He believes that every struggling business deserves a second chance: its TurnAround moment.
